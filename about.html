About us

The team behind the build

Saint Michael Web Services Limited is a London digital studio. We combine design, engineering and strategy to help businesses win online.

Our story

Founded on craft, run on trust

We started with a simple observation: too many businesses were paying for websites that looked fine and performed badly. Slow pages, broken mobile layouts, no measurement, and no one to call when something went wrong.

Saint Michael Web Services was built to fix that. We keep our team small and senior, we take on work we can genuinely do well, and we stay accountable long after launch day.

Today we work with startups, professional services firms and established retailers across the UK and beyond — from single landing pages to complete platform rebuilds.
